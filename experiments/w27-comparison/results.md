# W27 results — Pr(π ⊄ σ_n) across all patterns, deep tail, versus the identity

Method: SMC growth estimator (avoid2.c; ratio Pr(⊄ at n+1)/Pr(⊄ at n) = E_{σ ~ uniform π-avoider of length n}
[#safe insertion slots for a new maximum]/(n+1), population Monte Carlo with systematic resampling; the solver core is
W21's contain_bc). Validation: exact Av_n(π) for n ≤ 8 (OEIS values reproduced for 1234/1324/1342), exact LIS tails
(hook-length sums, lis_exact.py): identity k=4 pop 1e5 gives ln p = −13.914 at n = 24 vs exact −13.958; the three
non-identity members of the identity's Wilf class at k = 4 (1243, 1432, 2143) land within 0.04 of it at n = 24.
Noise ≈ ±0.04 in ln p at the deepest n for pop 1e5 (k=4), larger for the small-population scans (see per-k notes).
Numbers are ln p_π(n); "excess" = ln p_π(n) − ln p_id(n) (> 0 means π is HARDER to contain than the identity).

Notes on the deep runs. (a) 1 ⊕ dec_{k−2} ⊕ 1 is the hardest pattern at every k; its excess at n ≈ 1.33k² is
1.33 (k=6), 2.96 (k=7), 3.17 (k=8) and at n ≈ 1.5k² 4.04 (k=7), 3.91 (k=8): growing with k but sub-linearly in
this range, i.e. compatible with an e^{O(k)} error factor (and certainly not e^{Θ(k²)}); noise ±0.2–0.3 at the
deepest points. (b) Its local per-point rate is 8–15% below the identity's (0.92 vs 1.09 at k=6, 0.81 vs 0.94 at
k=7, 0.85 vs 0.92 at k=8): the tail is a genuinely slower exponential — CP(K) needs K > 1 — but the ratio of rates
is close to 1 and not visibly deteriorating with k. (c) Layered (21)^{k/2}: excess ≈ 0 up to n ≈ k², then mildly
positive (+0.5 at k=6, +1.05 at k=8, n = 1.12k²); local rate 1.05 vs 1.09 (k=6): as hard as the identity, in
agreement with W21. (d) Tilted 2-strip grids (12)^h-type: EASIER than the identity at every n (excess −3.4 at
k=7, n=72) with local rate 1.04–1.06 ≥ identity's: no CP issue. (e) The k = 8 grid runs (15263748, 13572468) were
still running at the time of writing; partial data are in deep/.
